# Pub Night Picker

## Language

**Group**:
A community to which Meets belong, forming a boundary for membership and administration.

**Meet**:
A gathering scheduled for a date, from its voting phase through to its completion and beyond.
_Avoid_: Event, poll as a name for the gathering

**Poll**:
The voting activity for a Meet.

**Voting Option**:
A choice in a Poll: a specific candidate Venue or an indication that any candidate Venue is acceptable.

**Vote**:
One user's selection of one Voting Option in a Poll. A user can select multiple Voting Options in the same Poll.

**Attendance Response**:
One user's indication of whether they can come to a Meet at a particular Venue, optionally including an estimated arrival time.

**Any-Venue Attendance Response**:
One user's indication of whether they can come to a Meet regardless of which main Venue is chosen. It does not indicate attendance at a restaurant.

**Venue**:
A place or event that can be a choice for a Meet, including a pub or restaurant.

**Location**:
A named area that groups Venues for discovery and selection, such as Cambridge, Histon or London.

**Event venue**:
A Venue representing an event, distinct from the Meet that may take place there.
