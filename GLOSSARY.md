# Pub Night Picker

## Language

**Meet**:
A gathering scheduled for a date, from its voting phase through to its completion and beyond.
_Avoid_: Event, poll as a name for the gathering

**Poll**:
The voting activity for a Meet.

**Venue**:
A place or event that can be a choice for a Meet, including a pub or restaurant.

**Event venue**:
A Venue representing an event, distinct from the Meet that may take place there.
